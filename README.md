# FAME
This repository contains the notes prepared by teaching fellows for Eli Fenichel's Fundamentals of Applied Math for Environmental Scientists Course at the Yale School of the Environment in the Fall of 2026, heavily inspired by Andie Creel's notes from Fall 2025 (See: https://github.com/a5creel/FAME). 

The repository has two primary folders, `class notes` and `section notes`. The class notes are taken during Eli's in-class lectures. The section notes contain supplemental materials from previous years' sections, written by Andie Creel.

Note: You can ignore the .tex files. The PDFs are what are viewable and what you want! The .tex files are just how I make the pdfs.
